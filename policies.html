Problem Sets

Homework

Homework is released weekly and submitted through Gradescope. Problem sets are the heart of the course — they are where the ideas from lecture become your own.

Submission

  • Solutions must be typeset (LaTeX strongly recommended) and submitted as a PDF.
  • Match each problem to its page in Gradescope when you submit.
  • Late submissions are handled by the late-day policy below.

Late days

Each student has a small budget of late days to use across the semester — typically up to two per assignment. Beyond your budget, late work receives no credit except in documented emergencies. Contact the instructors early if a serious situation arises.

Writing matters. A correct algorithm with an unclear or unjustified writeup will not receive full marks. Aim for proofs a classmate could read and be convinced by.

Exams

Midterms & Final

There are two midterm exams during the semester and a cumulative final during finals week. Exam dates are posted on the course calendar. Conflicts must be reported well in advance so an alternate sitting can be arranged.

What to expect

  • Closed-book, with a single hand-written reference sheet permitted.
  • Problems test understanding and problem-solving, not memorization.
  • The best preparation is doing (and re-doing) homework and recitation problems.

Getting Help

Communication & Support

  • Ed — the primary place for questions. Search first; post publicly when you can so others benefit.
  • Office hours — sign up on the OHQ; come with specific questions and your attempts so far.
  • Recitations — Fridays; the fastest way to build fluency with the week's material.
Accommodations. Students with disabilities are encouraged to contact the Office of Disability Resources and to notify the instructors early so we can arrange appropriate accommodations.
